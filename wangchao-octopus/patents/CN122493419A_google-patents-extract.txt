===== abstract 1396
 Abstract Translated from Chinese 本申请公开了一种基于深度相机与视觉大模型的厕具识别方法及系统，涉及厕具识别领域。首先对彩色图像与深度点云进行时空对齐融合，获取高质量的色彩深度对齐数据。随后，利用视觉大模型提取特征以获取二维锚框及类别置信度评分，并创新性地以二维锚框作为像素空间遮罩。接着，将类别置信度评分作为配准加权因子，对隔离出的三维点云进行姿态配准，避免配准陷入局部最优，从而输出高精度的六自由度位姿参数。最后，结合手眼标定矩阵与预设安全距离阈值进行防碰撞差分校验，计算出无碰撞作业坐标，彻底打通了从高精度多模态识别到机械臂安全自动化作业的技术闭环。 This application discloses a toilet recognition method and system based on a depth camera and a large visual model, relating to the field of toilet recognition. First, a color image and a depth point cloud are spatiotemporally aligned and fused to obtain high-quality color-depth aligned data. Then, features are extracted using a large visual model to obtain two-dimensional anchor boxes and category confidence scores, innovatively using the two-dimensional anchor boxes as pixel space masks. Next, the category confidence scores are used as registration weighting factors to perform pose registration on the isolated three-dimensional point cloud, avoiding registration getting trapped in local optima, thereby outputting high-precision six-DOF pose parameters. Finally, anti-collision differential verification is performed by combining the hand-eye calibration matrix and a preset safe distance threshold to calculate collision-free operating coordinates, completely establishing a technical closed loop from high-precision multimodal recognition to safe automated operation of the robotic arm.

===== description 102006
 Description Toilet appliance identification method and system based on depth camera and vision large model Technical Field The application relates to the field of toilet identification, in particular to a toilet identification method and system based on a depth camera and a visual large model. Background With rapid development of service robots and automation technologies, automatic cleaning and maintenance operations in complex scenes such as toilets are becoming a focus of attention in the industry. In an automatic operation task, various toilets such as a closestool, a urinal and the like are accurately identified and positioned, and the automatic operation device is a precondition for realizing accurate interaction and operation of a robot or a mechanical arm. However, the bathroom environment often has challenges such as severe illumination variation, narrow space, high reflectivity of ceramic surfaces, lack of significant textural features, and the like. The three-dimensional space position of the target cannot be obtained only by means of the traditional two-dimensional vision technology, the mechanical arm is difficult to guide to conduct depth operation, and the single three-dimensional point cloud is difficult to accurately analyze semantic information of a complex scene. Therefore, a toilet appliance identification scheme based on the depth camera and the large visual model is constructed, powerful generalized identification and semantic understanding capability of the large visual model are fused with high-precision three-dimensional space perception capability of the depth camera, and the method becomes a necessary development trend for endowing a service robot with comet eyes and realizing high-precision six-degree-of-freedom pose estimation and intelligent operation of the toilet appliance. However, existing toilet identification schemes based on depth cameras and visual models still have many drawbacks in practical applications. Firstly, in the multi-mode data processing stage, the existing method often lacks a strict space-time alignment and time deviation evaluation mechanism, so that time delay and space projection deviation exist between a color image and a depth point cloud, and high-quality fusion characteristics cannot be formed. Secondly, for the extraction of a toilet target, the detection precision of a conventional visual model is limited when a disordered background is processed, and the two-dimensional semantic anchor frame cannot be effectively utilized as a mask to guide the accurate cutting of the three-dimensional point cloud, so that the extracted three-dimensional point cloud contains a large amount of background noise points and adhesion matters, and the difficulty of subsequent segmentation and calculation is greatly increased. In addition, when three-dimensional pose estimation is performed, the conventional point cloud rigid matching is mostly adopted in the existing algorithm, and the category confidence coefficient of the visual model cannot be fully utilized as the registration weight, so that local optimization is easily involved when the point cloud is absent or has interference, and high-precision six-degree-of-freedom pose parameters cannot be acquired. Finally, most of the existing schemes stay on the pure vision coordinate output level, lack of depth combination with a robot hand-eye calibration system and an anti-collision safety mechanism, and cause that the mechanical arm is extremely easy to collide with and damage the fragile ceramic toilet due to coordinate calculation errors when the mechanical arm executes close operation. Therefore, a comprehensive toilet identification scheme capable of achieving space-time fusion, accurate denoising, intelligent registration and anti-collision verification is urgently needed in the industry. Disclosure of Invention Aiming at the problems in the prior art, the application provides a toilet identification method based on a depth camera and a vision large model, which comprises the steps of firstly, performing space-time alignment fusion on an acquired original sensing data stream based on an internal and external reference coordinate system to obtain toilet color depth alignment data, wherein the original sensing data stream comprises a toilet color image and a toilet depth point cloud, secondly, performing multi-layer convolution semantic feature extraction and anchor frame target detection on a color channel in the toilet color depth alignment data based on the vision large model to obtain a toilet two-dimensional anchor frame matrix and a toilet category confidence score, thirdly, performing interesting area point cloud clipping, statistical filtering and clustering segmentation on the depth channel in the toilet color depth alignment data to obtain toilet isolation three-dimensional point cloud, fourthly, performing main component analysis and iterative closest point gesture registration on the toilet isolation three-dimensional point cloud to obtain a toilet six-degree-of-freedom pose parameter, fifthly, and performing collision-free operation coordinate transformation on the three-dimensional coordinate system through the eye calibration three-dimensional coordinate transformation of the preset three-dimensional anchor frame matrix. The application further provides a toilet identification system based on the depth camera and the visual large model, which comprises a sensing data pre-alignment module, a pose parameter generation module, a semantic extraction target detection module and a three-dimensional point cloud generation module, wherein the sensing data pre-alignment module is used for carrying out space-time alignment fusion on an acquired original sensing data stream based on an internal parameter coordinate system and an external parameter coordinate system to obtain toilet color depth alignment data, the original sensing data stream comprises a toilet color image and a toilet depth point cloud, the semantic extraction target detection module is used for carrying out multi-layer convolution semantic feature extraction and anchor frame target detection on color channels in the toilet color depth alignment data based on the visual large model to obtain a toilet two-dimensional anchor frame matrix and a toilet category confidence score, the three-dimensional point cloud generation module is used for carrying out interesting region point cloud cutting, statistical filtering and clustering segmentation on the depth channels in the toilet color depth alignment data to obtain toilet isolation three-dimensional point cloud, the pose parameter generation module is used for carrying out main component analysis and nearest point pose registration on the toilet isolation three-dimensional point cloud based on the visual large model to obtain toilet six-degree-of-freedom coordinate system, and carrying out collision-free coordinate transformation on the three-dimensional coordinate system through the three-dimensional point cloud calibration module and the three-dimensional point cloud calibration module. Compared with the prior art, the toilet appliance identification method and system based on the depth camera and the vision large model provided by the application are used for solving the problems that in the prior art, multi-mode data fusion quality is poor, point cloud noise points are many, pose estimation accuracy is low, and mechanical arm operation is easy to collide. Firstly, carrying out space-time alignment fusion on the color image and the depth point cloud to obtain high-quality color depth alignment data, and effectively overcoming space-time deviation among multiple sensors. And then, extracting features by using a visual large model to obtain two-dimensional anchor frames and category confidence scores, innovatively taking the two-dimensional anchor frames as pixel space masks, guiding the depth image layer to perform accurate cutting and filtering noise reduction, and realizing efficient stripping of targets and clutter. And then, taking the category confidence score as a registration weighting factor, and carrying out gesture registration on the isolated three-dimensional point cloud to avoid the registration from being trapped into local optimum, thereby outputting high-precision six-degree-of-freedom pose parameters. And finally, combining the hand-eye calibration matrix and a preset safety distance threshold value to perform anti-collision differential verification, calculating a collision-free operation coordinate, and thoroughly opening a technical closed loop from high-precision multi-mode identification to mechanical arm safety automatic operation.
