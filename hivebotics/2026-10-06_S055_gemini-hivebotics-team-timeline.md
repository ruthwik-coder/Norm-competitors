HIVEBOTICS PTE. LTD. was legally incorporated on July 14, 2022 in Singapore.

The company transitioned from an academic incubator project at the National University of Singapore (NUS) to an internationally deployed commercial robotics vendor over the following key phases:

Chronological Development Timeline
Mid-2022 (Foundation & Incubation):
July 14, 2022: Officially incorporated as an exempt private company limited by shares in Singapore (UEN: 202224434W).
Originated from the NUS Graduate Research Innovation Programme (NUS GRIP), where co-founders Rishab Patwari and Nguyen Tuan Dung initially developed the concept alongside the NUS Advanced Robotics Centre.
Stationed at LaunchPad @ one-north (Ayer Rajah Crescent, Singapore).

2023 (R&D & The First Abluo Prototype):
Developed and tested the early proof-of-concept Abluo Mark 1 prototype.
Focused on the physical hard-environment challenges: multi-axis manipulator force-torque control, steam generation thermal circuits (~173°C), and navigation inside wet, narrow, specular bathroom cubicles.
Closed initial accelerator/pre-seed funding (~$244K).

2024 (Government Challenges & Multi-Country Pilots):
March 2024: Won the JTC Innovation Challenge 2023 ("Cleaning toilets with minimal human intervention") awarded by Jurong Town Corporation, unlocking Singapore government testbeds.
Mid-to-Late 2024: Rolled out pilot trials across 5 territories (Singapore, Hong Kong, UAE/Abu Dhabi, EU, and the US).
Piloted high-traffic deployments, including Changi Airport transit hubs and tertiary hospital restrooms.
Hired key commercial leadership, including Vincent Sim (former Managing Director of Kärcher Singapore) to lead go-to-market strategy.

2025 (Product Maturity & Abluo Mark 2):
Reached task-level validation for multi-surface sanitization (integrating steam, vacuum, and air-blade drying into a unified toolhead).
Raised a $2.5M seed extension/bridge round to scale hardware assembly and launch the hot-swappable battery architecture.
Deployed their public, live telemetry fleet dashboard tracking bowl counts, machine health, and cleaning completion scores.
Reached unit-economics break-even on early commercial hardware batches by Q4 2025.

2026 (Institutional Scale & Series A):
September 4, 2026: Raised a US$6.0 Million Series A round led by Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India.
Capital allocated to moving Abluo from limited pilot batches into large-scale production, expanding regional distribution networks across four primary markets, and staffing the team to ~18 full-time personnel.
