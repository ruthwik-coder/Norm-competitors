| Round | **US$6M Series A**, closed; announced **4 Sep 2026** |
| Investors | **Lead: Vertex Ventures SEA & India.** Also: **Fareast Land Development (Farglory Group)** and **Rigel** ("a leading Asian manufacturer of eco-friendly, smart restroom products") |
| Use of funds | Abluo from pilots → **volume production**; expand **distributor network** across **4 core markets**; develop **HiveIntelligence**, the proprietary "physical AI layer that plans, monitors and validates every cleaning job" |
| Product | Mobile robot with an **articulated arm**; cleans toilets, urinals, sinks and floors |
| Traction | "nearly **10,000 operating hours** over **12 months**", **more than 20 sites** in Asia, Europe, the Middle East and North America: hospitals, airport terminals, shopping malls |

| Cleaning method | "**cleans without contact**": high-pressure steam, a targeted chemical jet, vacuum extraction and a blow-drying stage, with an arm reaching under rims. Explicitly **no brushes or cloths** (cross-contamination risk) |
| Perception / verification | "Onboard vision identifies each fixture", an AI pipeline generates the cleaning route in real time, and "**every job ends with a scored before-and-after check**", so outcomes "can be audited rather than assumed" |
| Founding | "**founded in 2021** out of the National University of Singapore by **Rishab Patwari and Nguyen Tuan Dung**" |
