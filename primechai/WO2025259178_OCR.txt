TECHNICAL FIELD
[0001] This invention relates to a cleaning device. More particularly, this invention relates
to a cleaning robot for use in the cleaning of an environment.
BACKGROUND
[0002] The following discussion of the background to the invention is intended to facilitate
an understanding of one or more inventions disclosed by the present disclosure only. It should
be appreciated that the discussion is not an acknowledgement or admission that any of the
material referred to was published, known or part of the common general knowledge of the
person skilled in the art in any jurisdiction as at the priority date of one or more inventions
disclosed by the present disclosure.
[0003] An environment (for example a lavatory) may be cleaned by a cleaning robot that
can be operated by a human operator or be programmed to automatically clean the
environment. Cleaning robots may be developed for specific tasks such as cleaning floors,
cleaning toilet bowls and cleaning walls, where such robots may be equipped with specific tools
and/or functions that allow them to appropriately perform the tasks that they have been
developed for.
[0004] By way of an example, the fixtures and fittings in a lavatory environment create
areas that are difficult to access and clean, thereby allowing bacteria to thrive. Additionally,
stubborn stains commonly occur in toilet bowls that are difficult to remove. Most cleaning robots
can only perform floor cleaning which is a two-dimensional process. The cleaning of structures
in a lavatory, such as a urinal, a toilet bowl, a basin and a mirror requires a robot that can
perform three-dimensional cleaning with different tools. Additionally, using clean water alone to
clean lavatory structures especially those with stubborn stains, which known cleaning robots
do, is insufficient to achieve the sterilization and disinfection appropriate for the environment.
Supplementing the clean water with cleaning chemicals is not eco-friendly. While there are
cleaning robots that use a robotic arm to grip and hold a water gun for spraying water when
cleaning, the cleaning process performed by such robots is typically long due to the constant
need for robotic arm to swap between water gun and other cleaning tools.
