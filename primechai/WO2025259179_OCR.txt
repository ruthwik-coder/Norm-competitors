A SYSTEM AND METHOD FOR MAKING A FLUID

TECHNICAL FIELD
[0001] This invention relates to a system and method for making a fluid connection
between a cleaning robot and a base station. More particularly, this invention relates to
a system and method for connecting the cleaning robot to the base station for
transferring fluid therebetween.
BACKGROUND
[0002] The following discussion of the background to the invention is intended to
facilitate an understanding of the present invention only. It should be appreciated that
the discussion is not an acknowledgement or admission that any of the material
referred to was published, known or part of the common general knowledge of the
person skilled in the art in any jurisdiction as at the priority date of the invention.
[0003] Cleaning robots are widely used these days. Some of these robots include a
clean water tank and a dirty or waste water tank that require refilling and draining
respectively during use. Consequently, to increase the level of automation, base
stations are provided where the cleaning robot can move to dock at to have its clean
water tank refilled and waste water tank drained. An example of such a cleaning robot
and base station system is disclosed in Chinese Utility Model No. CN220024922U, Lee
et al., entitled “Water changing base station, intelligent cleaning machine and system
thereof’. The disclosed base station includes ports for connecting to corresponding
ports of the cleaning robot. The system uses blind docking between the cleaning robot
and the base station, which is low in docking accuracy and there is also a risk of
docking failure. The ports of the base station also protrude from an outer surface of a
housing of the base station. As a result, these protruding ports increase the footprint of
the base station. They are also a safety hazard. Furthermore, each port uses an
elastic sealing member sleeved thereon to seal the connection between two ports. The
two ports are forced to merely abut each other. There is a risk that water may leak out
of where the two ports meet.

[0004] There is therefore a need for a system for transferring fluid between a
cleaning robot and a base station which addresses, at least in part, one or more of the

SUMMARY
[0005] According to an aspect of the present disclosure, there is provided a method
for making a fluid connection between a cleaning robot and a base station. The
method includes moving the cleaning robot to the base station and picking up a first
connecting member from the base station using a robotic arm. The robotic arm may on
the cleaning robot or the base station. The method further includes connecting the first
connecting member to a second connecting member of the cleaning robot to complete
the fluid connection between the cleaning robot and the base station, and transferring
fluid between the cleaning robot and the base station through the fluid connection.
[0006] In some embodiments of the method, the method further includes extending
the second connecting member out of the cleaning robot before connecting the first
connecting member to the second connecting member.
[0007] In some embodiments of the method, the second connecting member is
extended through a trap door out of the cleaning robot.
[0008] In some embodiments of the method, the method further includes retracting
the second connecting member into the cleaning robot after transferring fluid between
the cleaning robot and the base station.
[0009] In some embodiments of the method, the method further includes detecting
that the fluid connection is established before transferring fluid between the cleaning
robot and the base station.

the cleaning robot to be detected at the first electrical contact of the cleaning robot.

contacts are location on the second connecting member of the cleaning robot.
