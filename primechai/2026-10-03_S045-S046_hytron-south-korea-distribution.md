## S046: Article text (as pasted)

Primech AI Pte. Ltd., a subsidiary of Primech Holdings, has announced a significant expansion into the South Korean market with its HYTRON cleaning robot. This strategic move is facilitated by a new distribution agreement projected to generate approximately US$4.08 million in revenue over three years. The partnership establishes a crucial commercial footprint for the company in one of Asia's most advanced automation economies.

A Strategic Partnership for Market Entry
Under the terms of the agreement, a local partner will act as Primech AI's authorized distributor in South Korea. This entity will spearhead localized marketing, sales, deployment, and technical support for the HYTRON system. The collaboration is designed to accelerate commercial adoption and build a framework for scalable deployment throughout the region.

The market entry follows a structured, phased rollout that commences with an initial trial period. Upon successful market validation, the distributor may transition to an exclusive multi-year arrangement with defined minimum order commitments. This approach provides a clear and methodical pathway to achieving scaled deployment and long-term market penetration.

Financial Projections and Commercial Strategy
The financial terms of the partnership are based on agreed-upon minimum order quantities and specific unit pricing. This structure is expected to yield cumulative revenues of around US$4.08 million over the initial three-year term. The revenue stream will begin with an initial trial order, followed by contractually increasing annual commitments.

Charles Ng, Chief Operating Officer of Primech AI, emphasized the move's importance for the company's international commercialization efforts. He stated that the structured distribution model and a capable local partner position Primech AI to capitalize on a market highly receptive to automation. This foundation is critical for achieving long-term, scalable growth in the smart facilities sector.

Tapping into South Korea's Advanced Automation Market
South Korea presents a highly attractive market for robotics due to its strong technology adoption and increasing demand for productivity-enhancing solutions. The country's advanced infrastructure and focus on innovation create an ideal environment for introducing sophisticated automation. Primech AI aims to leverage these conditions to establish a strong and scalable in-market presence.

The local distributor will play a pivotal role in navigating the market by managing local certification requirements and providing on-the-ground customer support. This localized expertise is expected to streamline market entry and accelerate deployment timelines significantly. It also ensures that customers receive consistent operational performance and reliable service delivery from the outset.

The HYTRON Solution for Modern Facilities
The HYTRON robot leverages advanced artificial intelligence and automation to deliver consistent and high-quality restroom cleaning in commercial environments. Its technology is engineered to perform tasks efficiently, ensuring a superior level of hygiene. This allows for standardized cleaning outcomes that are difficult to achieve with manual labor alone.

By automating a critical facility management task, HYTRON helps organizations address persistent labor constraints and rising operational costs. The system not only enhances productivity but also improves overall hygiene standards, a key priority for modern commercial properties. This dual benefit makes it a compelling solution for facility managers seeking to innovate their operations.

This expansion into South Korea represents a pivotal milestone in Primech AI's global strategy for its HYTRON robotic solution. The partnership not only secures a projected revenue stream but also establishes a vital foothold in the competitive Asian automation landscape. It effectively positions the company for sustained growth and broader international market penetration in the coming years.
